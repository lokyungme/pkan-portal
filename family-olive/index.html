01 / EARLY SIGNS

When did you first become concerned about your child's development?

Around 18 months, we noticed that Olive was falling more frequently than other children her age. She had started walking at 13 months, but seemed increasingly unsteady, especially when turning or walking on uneven surfaces. At first, we thought she was just finding her balance. By 21 months, we noticed that her right foot sometimes turned inward when she walked. We brought these concerns to her pediatrician, who referred us to a pediatric neurologist.

02 / CHANGES OVER TIME

How have your child's symptoms changed over time?

Between 18 and 24 months, Olive's balance became less consistent. She began falling several times a day, particularly when tired. Around 22 months, we noticed stiffness in her right leg, especially after sitting for long periods. She could still walk independently, but began needing help with stairs. We also noticed that her movements seemed more difficult toward the end of the day. We started keeping a weekly record of her walking, falls, and muscle stiffness to discuss with her neurologist.

03 / TREATMENT EXPERIENCES

What treatments or therapies has your child received?

Olive began physical therapy twice a week at 22 months, focusing on balance, stretching, and maintaining mobility. We also started occupational therapy once a week to work on coordination and everyday activities. Her neurologist recommended monitoring muscle stiffness before considering medication. We have been documenting how she responds to therapy, including changes in walking, fatigue, and her ability to participate in daily activities.
