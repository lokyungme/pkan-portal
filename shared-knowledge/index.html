Understanding PKAN, Together

Every family affected by PKAN has experiences and knowledge that could be valuable to others. From the earliest signs of the disease to changes in symptoms and responses to treatment, families gather information over months and years that often remains scattered across medical records, conversations, and personal notes.

Shared Knowledge is a place for families to bring this information together. By sharing our experiences, we can begin to see patterns, learn from one another, and contribute to a larger understanding of PKAN.

03 / CARE + CONTROL

Families should decide what they share.

The long-term goal is an open-source platform, not a publicly accessible collection of children's medical records. Families would control their contributions, and access to sensitive information would require meaningful consent and security.

01

Choose what to contribute

Not every experience needs to be shared. Families could add, review, and withdraw information.

02

Choose who can see it

Private records, verified family access, and approved research uses would have distinct permissions.

03

Build with families

Families would help shape the questions, the language, and the rules for how knowledge is used.
